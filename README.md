## How to get the files

**To read a guide,** click **Read it**. It opens right here in your browser, formatted and ready to follow.

**To copy the add-ons,** open the guide and find the gray box. Hover over it and click the copy icon in its top right corner. Paste it at the end of your project instructions.

**To download a guide as a Markdown file** (the best format for uploading to ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini), open the guide and click the **Download raw file** button. It's the download arrow icon at the top right of the file.

**To download the Word version,** click **Download** in the table. Word and PowerPoint files download straight to your computer, and you open them in Word, PowerPoint, Google Docs, or Google Slides.

## Where to start

1. Read the **Automation** guide and pick a combo.
2. Connect one tool. Get it working before you add another.
3. Paste the add-ons for your tools into your project instructions.
4. Say "Schedule my approved posts on our days and times."
5. Want to network on X too? Read the bonus guide.

Prices and plans change often. Check each vendor's page before you buy.
